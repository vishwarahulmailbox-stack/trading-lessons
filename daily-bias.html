Daily Bias

Daily bias is where the market is most likely to move today. It is a plan, not a prediction: a direction, a place to act, a target, and the exact point where you admit you were wrong.

1A plan, not a prediction

📓 From your notes

Daily Bias: most likely where the market will move on daily. It is plan, not prediction. Bias is a plan + invalidation, not a prediction.

A prediction says "EUR/USD will go up today" and leaves you emotionally attached. A plan says "EUR/USD is bullish; I buy in discount at my POI; if price closes below X my idea is wrong and I stop". A plan survives being wrong. A prediction does not.

2The 9 steps to build your daily bias

  1. 1Mark the higher timeframe trend, plus the swing high and swing lowDaily and 4H structure: HH/HL or LH/LL? Lesson 5.
  2. 2Find the range and see whether price is at discount or premiumDraw the 50% line of the current swing. Lesson 9.
  3. 3Look to buy at discount and sell at premiumOnly in the direction of step 1.
  4. 4Mark the liquidity where the money is restingGenerally yesterday's high and low (PDH, PDL), plus equal highs and lows.
  5. 5Price will move to the next nearest liquidity poolThat pool is your draw on liquidity and your target.
  6. 6Build the story: what is price doing?Continuing, pulling back, or consolidating?
  7. 7Trade with the HTFWhat is price currently doing on the higher timeframe? Do not fight it.
  8. 8Then look at the LTFOnly once price reaches your POI, check what the lower timeframe is doing. Lesson 7.
  9. 9If price does not follow the bias, stop and re-evaluateInvalidation hit = reset. No coping, no revenge trades.

5Exit rules

📓 From your notes

Exit rule: Yesterday's high or low where liquidity is sitting. At zones, supply or demand. At the imbalance / FVG. Swing high | swing low.

Yesterday's high / low

The most common daily target: the liquidity resting at PDH or PDL.

Opposing zones

Buying? Exit before the next supply zone. Selling? Exit before the next demand zone.

Imbalance / FVG

An unfilled FVG in your direction acts as a magnet and a natural place for partial profits.

Swing high / low

The weak high (buys) or weak low (sells) of the current structure.

6Five mistakes with daily bias

📓 From your notes

5 mistakes for daily bias: 1) They try to find daily bias at 5M TF, there is lot of noise. Go for HTF 4H. 2) Involve feeling, emotion are not strategy. 3) They trade mid range, choppy. 4) Changing bias every candle, change only if the bias is invalidated. 5) Not trusting your bias.

MistakeWhy it hurtsFix
1. Building bias on the 5MToo much noise; every few candles look like a new trendUse the 4H and Daily for bias
2. Trading feelingsEmotion is not a strategyWrite the bias statement before the session opens
3. Trading mid rangeEquilibrium is choppy, no edgeAct only in discount (buys) or premium (sells)
4. Flip-flopping every candleYou end up buying tops and selling bottomsChange only when the invalidation level is hit
5. Not trusting your biasYou skip the valid setup, then chase laterBuild trust with journal data (see Mindset)
✅ Key takeaways
  • Bias = plan + invalidation. Never a prediction.
  • Build it on 4H / Daily: trend → premium/discount → liquidity → story.
  • Target yesterday's high/low, opposing zones, FVGs or weak swing points.
  • If invalidated, stop and re-evaluate. Do not change bias on every candle.
