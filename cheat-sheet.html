1The full process, top to bottom

  1. 1Before the session: HTF story Daily / 4HTrend (HH/HL or LH/LL)? Swing high and low? Premium or discount? Where is liquidity (PDH, PDL, equal highs/lows)? Any major zone, OB or FVG nearby? Check the news calendar.
  2. 2Write the daily bias in one sentenceDirection + where I act + target + invalidation.
  3. 3MTF: mark the POI 4H / 1H / 15MA valid OB, FVG, supply/demand or flip zone, in the correct half of the range. Set an alert. Close the LTF.
  4. 4Wait for the kill zone and for price to reach the POINo POI reached = no trade. "There will always be a next setup."
  5. 5LTF: confirmation 5M / 1MLiquidity sweep → CHoCH → FVG. Enter on the pullback.
  6. 6Run the 5 question processMy setup? Where is SL? How much risk? Would I take it ignoring P&L? Am I okay if it loses?
  7. 7Execute with fixed riskSL beyond the zone, TP before the next liquidity. Position size from the calculator.
  8. 8Manage, then journalDo not move the stop away. Journal the trade, screenshot, emotion, rules followed.

2Golden rules from the notebook

Don't trade ego or pride. Only trade price.

Enter in the balance state of price and capture the imbalance.

Expect a pullback after a BOS.

Trade from strong structure, target weak structure.

Whatever happens on the HTF must happen first on the LTF.

HTF candles matter more than LTF candles. Price is the live indicator.

The stronger the move away from a zone, the more important the zone.

Buy in discount, sell in premium, in line with the trend.

An FVG alone is not a setup.

A valid OB needs all 5 rules.

If you can't spot the liquidity, you are the liquidity.

Never look at the LTF until price reaches your MTF POI.

Bias is a plan + invalidation, not a prediction.

Build trust in your system with data, not affirmations.

Process first, profit second.

Best time to trade is when a setup meets your criteria.
