Trading Styles & Timeframes

Scalping, intraday, swing or position trading: each uses different charts, different targets and suits a different life. Here is how to pick one, and how to choose a broker.

1The four styles

💡 Why two tables?

The first table (styles) lists the charts you mostly watch while in a trade. The second (stacks) lists the full top down sequence you analyse before a trade. A swing trader manages the trade on 1H to 4H, but plans it from the Daily and fine-tunes the entry on 15M.

3Which one should you choose?

📓 From your notes

Which to choose: 1) Fit into your schedule 2) Aligns with goals 3) Suits your personality.

🗓

Fits your schedule

If you work 9 to 5, scalping the London open is not realistic. Pick a style whose key hours you can actually be at the screen.

🎯

Aligns with your goals

Steady growth of a long-term account suits swing. Building skill quickly with many repetitions suits intraday.

🧬

Suits your personality

Impatient people hate holding for days; anxious people hate fast scalps. Fighting your nature leads to broken rules.

4Experiment → Define → Iterate

  1. 1ExperimentTry a style on a demo account for a few weeks. Log every trade in a journal: pair, time, setup, result, how you felt.
  2. 2DefineWrite down the rules that worked: which session, which timeframes, which setups, what risk.
  3. 3IterateReview the journal weekly. Change one thing at a time, then test again. The data, not your mood, decides.

✅ Key takeaways
  • Higher timeframe styles = bigger targets, longer holds, fewer trades, wider stops.
  • Choose the style that fits your schedule, goals and personality, then prove it with data.
  • Each style uses an HTF / MTF / LTF stack for analysis.
  • Your broker holds your money: treat choosing one as a risk decision.
