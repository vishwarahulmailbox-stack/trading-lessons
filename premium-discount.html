Premium & Discount

Split any swing in half. The top half is expensive, the bottom half is cheap. Smart money buys cheap and sells expensive. This one idea filters out a huge number of bad trades.

  • The idea
  • Try it
  • How to draw it
  • With the trend
  • Going deeper

1Cheap and expensive

📓 From your notes

Always buy, when the price is in 50% Discount Range. Always sell, when the price is in 50% Premimum Range.

🛒 Analogy

A pair of shoes swung between $60 and $100 this year. At $95 you would wait for a sale. At $65 you would buy. You do not know the "right" price, but you know where the cheap half and the expensive half are. Premium and discount apply the same logic to every swing on the chart.

2Try it: where is price now?

3How to draw it

  1. 1Pick the swing (dealing range)In an uptrend, from the swing low that started the last leg up to the latest swing high. In a downtrend, from the swing high to the swing low.
  2. 2Use the Fibonacci toolDraw from low to high (uptrend). Keep only the levels 0, 0.5 and 1 to keep it clean. Many traders add 0.62 and 0.79 (see below).
  3. 3Read the halvesBelow 0.5 = discount. Above 0.5 = premium. Exactly 0.5 = equilibrium.
  4. 4Redraw after a new BOSWhen structure breaks, a new swing exists, so the range moves.
// Equilibrium (50%) of a swing EQ = (Swing High + Swing Low) ÷ 2 // example: high 1.1000, low 1.0800 EQ = (1.1000 + 1.0800) ÷ 2 = 1.0900 → buys below 1.0900, sells above

4"Always" means: in line with the trend

🛠 Important nuance

The rule "always buy in discount" works when the higher timeframe is bullish. In a strong downtrend, price will sit in discount for a long time and keep falling. The full rule is: decide the direction with structure first, then only take that direction from the correct half.

HTF trendPrice in discountPrice in premium
BullishLook for buys (OBs, demand, FVGs)Do not buy. Wait. Maybe take profit.
BearishDo not sell. Wait. Maybe take profit.Look for sells (OBs, supply, FVGs)
RangingBuy near range low (with confirmation)Sell near range high (with confirmation)

Your notes connect premium and discount to many other rules: a valid order block must be in discount (buys) or premium (sells), the 50% of a zone rule uses the same idea, and the daily bias process says "buy at discount, sell at premium". It also explains the rule "do not trade mid range": at equilibrium you have no price advantage either way.

5Going deeper: the optimal trade entry

💡 Not in your notes, but widely used

Many smart money traders look for entries in the 0.62 to 0.79 area of the swing (often called the Optimal Trade Entry, OTE). It is deep enough in discount to give a great price, but not so deep that the swing low is likely to break. Combine it with an OB or FVG sitting in that area.

✅ Key takeaways
  • Split the current swing at 50%. Lower half = discount, upper half = premium.
  • In an uptrend, buy only in discount. In a downtrend, sell only in premium.
  • Avoid trading at equilibrium (mid range).
  • Redraw the range after every new break of structure.
