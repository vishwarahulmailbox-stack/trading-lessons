Beliefs & Mindset

Your notes mark this "very important", and for good reason. Most traders who fail had a decent method; they just could not follow it. This lesson turns beliefs into habits and habits into a process.

1Five beliefs that drive behaviour

📓 From your notes

Belief ⇒ make you believe in yourself. I am becoming consistent by following my rules, one trade at a time. I am disciplined. My job is execution, money is by-product. There will always be next step. I manage risk, not need certainty.

"I am becoming consistent by following my rules, one trade at a time."

Consistency is not a result, it is a behaviour. You measure it by "did I follow my rules on this trade?", not by "did I win?".

"I am disciplined."

Discipline means doing the boring, correct thing: waiting for the setup, taking the stop, skipping the trade that does not qualify.

"My job is execution, money is a by-product."

You control the process, the market controls the outcome. A good trade can lose and a bad trade can win. Judge the execution.

"There will always be a next setup."

Missing a move is not a loss. The fear of missing out is what makes people chase. The market is open five days a week, every week.

"I manage risk, I don't need certainty."

No setup is certain. Professionals think in probabilities: a defined, small loss when wrong, and a bigger win when right.

You cannot control whether the next trade wins. You can always control whether you followed your rules.

2Don't trade ego or pride, only trade price

Ego shows up in recognisable ways. Catch yourself when you notice any of these thoughts:

Ego thoughtWhat it leads toPrice-based replacement
"I know it will turn here."Moving or removing the stop loss"My invalidation was hit. The idea is wrong. Exit."
"I need to win back that loss."Revenge trading, bigger size"The last trade is closed. Reset, same risk."
"Everyone is buying, I'll miss out."Chasing an expansion with no zone"No POI, no trade. There will be another setup."
"I was right, the market is wrong."Holding losers, cutting winners"Price is the only truth on the chart."

3You build trust with data, not affirmations

📓 From your notes (Trading Notes)

Bias is a plan + invalidation, not a prediction. If you can't say your bias in one statement, you don't have one. If invalidation hits, reset. No coping, no revenge trades. You don't build trust in your system with affirmations. You build it with data.

There is no contradiction between the beliefs above and this note. The beliefs tell you how to behave. The data from your journal tells you whether the method works. After 50 to 100 trades taken exactly by your rules, you know your real win rate and average reward. That number, not a motivational sentence, is what lets you take the next trade without fear.

5The pre-trade process

📓 From your notes · 7 Sep

Process: 1) Is this my setup? 2) Where is my stop loss? 3) How much am I risking? 4) Would I trade without looking at P&L? 5) Am I okay if this trade loses? Process first, profit second.

Run this checklist before every single trade. Tick a box only if the honest answer is yes.

✅ Key takeaways
  • Your job is execution. Judge yourself on following rules, not on one result.
  • Ego moves stops and chases price; trade only what price shows.
  • Beliefs guide behaviour; journal data builds real confidence.
  • Run the 5 question process before every trade. Any "no" means no trade.
