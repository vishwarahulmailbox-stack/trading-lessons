Kill Zones

Kill zones are the windows of the day when big players are active and the best setups appear. Trading inside them, and resting outside them, is one of the simplest edges you can add.

🛠 Important correction: these are New York times, not UTC

Your notes label the hours as UTC, but these are the standard kill zone times in New York time (ET). In UTC they are 4 to 5 hours later (depending on US daylight saving). For example, the London kill zone 02:00 to 05:00 New York is 07:00 to 10:00 UTC in summer. The table below converts everything to your own time zone automatically, based on your device clock.

2The four kill zones

1. Asia · 20:00 to 00:00 NY

AUD, NZD and JPY pairs. Low volume. USD pairs tend to consolidate, building the Asian range. Catch 15 to 20 pips. Its high and low become liquidity for London.

2. London · 02:00 to 05:00 NY ★ favourite

EUR and GBP pairs. High volume. Catch 20 to 50 pips. Often creates the high or low of the day by sweeping one side of the Asian range.

3. New York · 07:00 to 10:00 NY

USD pairs. High activity and volume, US news. Often retraces into the London range and then continues with the swing structure. Catch 20 to 50 pips.

4. London close · 10:00 to 12:00 NY

USD pairs. Volume dropping as Europe goes home. Often retraces back into the daily range as traders take profit. Catch 15 to 20 pips.

3The London pattern

📓 From your notes

Price sweeps Asian high, creates high for day, then goes down → Bearish daily structure. Bearish... Bullish daily structure: price sweeps Asian low, creates a low for the day, then goes up.

Daily structure (HTF bias)Expect in LondonYour trade
BullishSweep of the Asian low → low of the day → rallyBuy after the sweep + LTF CHoCH, target Asian high and beyond
BearishSweep of the Asian high → high of the day → dropSell after the sweep + LTF CHoCH, target Asian low and beyond
💡 Why it works

This is liquidity in action. The Asian range is obvious to everyone, so stops pile up on both sides. London's big orders need that fuel. The first move is often the fake one (sometimes called a "Judas swing"); the second is the real one, in the direction of the daily bias.

4How to use kill zones

  • Plan outside, execute inside. Do your top down and daily bias before the kill zone. Use the kill zone only for execution.
  • Pick one or two. Choose the kill zones that fit your life and pairs. You do not need all four.
  • Mark the Asian range every day. Its high and low are your first liquidity levels for London.
  • Watch the news calendar. High impact news inside New York can make the kill zone violent (see When not to trade).
  • Outside kill zones, step away. Lunchtime and late-day chop is where most overtrading happens.
✅ Key takeaways
  • Kill zone times are in New York time; use the table above for your local time.
  • London (02:00 to 05:00 NY) is the favourite: high volume, often sets the high or low of the day.
  • Bullish day: London sweeps the Asian low then rallies. Bearish day: sweeps the Asian high then drops.
  • Plan before the kill zone, execute inside it.
