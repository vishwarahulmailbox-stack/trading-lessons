Market Structure

The foundation of the pyramid. Market structure tells you who is in control, where the trend is going, and when that is changing. Every other concept is read in the context of structure.

2Swing vocabulary

TermMeaning
Swing highA peak with lower prices on both sides. A turning point where sellers took over.
Swing lowA trough with higher prices on both sides. A turning point where buyers took over.
HH / HLHigher high / higher low: each peak and each trough is above the previous one.
LH / LLLower high / lower low: each peak and trough is below the previous one.
BOSBreak of structure: price closes beyond the last swing point in the direction of the trend. Confirms continuation.
CHoCH / MSSChange of character / market structure shift: price breaks the last swing point against the trend. First sign of reversal.

💡 Quick test

Cover the right half of the chart with your hand. Can you say in one word whether the left half is "up", "down" or "sideways"? If you need more than one word, zoom out to a higher timeframe until you can.

4Break of structure and pullbacks

📓 From your notes

Rule: Pullback happens after BOS. Expect a pullback after a Break of Structure.

Why is a pullback expected? When price breaks a high, early buyers take profit and late sellers get stopped out. The move pauses and retraces to rebalance (fill imbalances, test a zone) before the next leg. That retracement is your entry opportunity: you buy the higher low, not the breakout candle.

⚠️ Body close vs wick

Only count a BOS when a candle closes beyond the swing level. A wick that pokes through and closes back inside is usually a liquidity sweep, the opposite signal.

5Internal structure

📓 From your notes

Internal structure is anything happening in between swing High & swing Low. Whatever happens on HTF must happen first in LTF.

"Whatever happens on HTF must happen first on LTF" is pure logic: a 4H candle is built from 15M candles. Before the 4H chart can show a reversal, the 15M chart must already have broken its own internal structure. That is why LTF confirmation lets you enter early, at a better price, with a tighter stop.

6Trade from strong structure, target weak structure

📓 From your notes

Trade from strong structure & target weak structure. Strong structure: which has supported price, ignited. Weak structure: which has pushed price away.

Strong structure

A swing point that ignited a move strong enough to break structure. Big players defended it. In an uptrend the strong lows are where you look to buy.

Weak structure

A swing point that has not produced a break yet: price was just pushed away from it. Stops gather beyond it, so it is likely to be taken. That makes it your target.

7Market shifts: Change of Character (CHoCH)

📓 From your notes

Before deep analysis, first find out: 1) Is market in uptrend or downtrend? 2) Where are highs & lows? 3) Is the move a pullback or reversal? 4) Who is in control now?

BOSCHoCH
DirectionWith the trendAgainst the trend
Which level breaksLast high in an uptrend (last low in a downtrend)Last higher low in an uptrend (last lower high in a downtrend)
MessageTrend continues: same side in controlControl may be changing hands
What you doWait for the pullback, enter with trendStop looking for trend trades; wait for the new structure to confirm

Pullback or reversal?

This is the most common question on any chart. A simple rule: as long as the last strong low holds (uptrend), every drop is a pullback. Once price closes below that strong low, it is a CHoCH and possibly a reversal. A single CHoCH on a low timeframe inside a higher timeframe uptrend is often just a deeper pullback, which is why you always check the HTF first.

✅ Key takeaways
  • Uptrend = HH + HL. Downtrend = LH + LL. Range = equal highs and lows.
  • BOS confirms the trend; expect a pullback after it, and enter on that pullback.
  • Count breaks with candle closes, not wicks.
  • Trade from strong structure (it caused a break), target weak structure (it has liquidity).
  • CHoCH is the first warning that control is shifting.
