Flip Zones

A flip zone shows a change in control. Old supply gets broken and turns into new demand (or old demand turns into supply). It is where a reversal gives you its first, best quality entry.

  • What it is
  • How to find one
  • Validity rules
  • The trade plan

1What is a flip zone?

📓 From your notes

Flip zones: shows who's in control. It shows change in control. 1) Supply flip zone: flips the supply. 2) Demand flip zone: flips the demand. Determine the flip zone & place trade when price reach in the zone again.

Supply flip (bullish)

Sellers defended a level. Then buyers broke through it. Sellers are now trapped; the area flips into demand. Look to buy the return.

Demand flip (bearish)

Buyers defended a level. Then sellers broke below it. Buyers are now trapped; the area flips into supply. Look to sell the return.

🏠 Analogy

A floor becomes a ceiling and a ceiling becomes a floor. Once a team breaks into the other team's half, the old defensive line becomes their new front line.

2How to find a flip zone

📓 From your notes

How to find flip zones: 1) Look for market shift (change of character). 2) Break the demand / supply zone. Rule: should not break the High or Low of zone. Should have the BOS to confirm.

3Validity rules

📓 From your notes

Valid flip zones: 1) Price must react to supply for flip to be valid. 2) Reaction fails to create LL. Draw flip zone from top to bottom of reaction (or refine to pivot candle). 3) Price MUST break & close above the reaction point, break structure.

4The trade plan

  1. 1Spot the shiftA trend fails to continue and makes a CHoCH.
  2. 2Draw the zoneThe reaction that caused the break, from top to bottom. Refine to the pivot candle for a tighter stop.
  3. 3Wait for the returnDo not chase the breakout candle. Let price pull back into the zone (the "pullback after BOS" rule).
  4. 4Enter with confirmationOptional LTF CHoCH inside the zone. Stop beyond the zone's far edge.
  5. 5Target liquidityThe old highs left behind by the previous trend are the natural targets.
💡 Why flip zones are powerful

They combine three lessons at once: a CHoCH (control changed), a zone (where the new side entered), and trapped traders (the sellers who sold at ① now have stops above, which fuels the move up).

✅ Key takeaways
  • A flip zone is a broken supply that becomes demand (or the reverse): control changed hands.
  • Valid only if price reacted, failed to make a new extreme, and closed beyond the reaction point.
  • Trade it on the return, with the stop beyond the zone.
