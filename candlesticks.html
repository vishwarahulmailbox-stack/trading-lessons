Japanese Candlesticks & Heikin Ashi

Every candle is a small story of a battle between buyers and sellers. Learn to read the body and the wicks, why higher timeframe candles matter more, and when the smoothed Heikin Ashi candle helps.

1Anatomy of a candle

A candle covers one period: one minute on the 1M chart, one day on the Daily. The body shows where price opened and closed: who won the period. The wicks (shadows) show how far price travelled and got rejected: where one side tried and failed.

2Compare the body with the wick: it matters

📓 From your notes

1) Compare body with wick, it matters.

What you seeWhat it tells you
Big body, small wicksStrong conviction. One side controlled the whole period. Often the start of an expansion or an imbalance.
Small body, long wicks both sidesIndecision. Balance. Both sides tried and failed.
Long wick on one sideRejection. Price went there and was pushed back hard. At a zone or liquidity level, this is a strong clue.
Bodies getting smaller in a trendMomentum fading. Possible consolidation or reversal coming.
⚠️ Context first

A hammer in the middle of nowhere means very little. The same hammer after a sweep of an old low, inside a demand zone, in discount, is a powerful signal. Your second rule says it: never lose the context.

3Heikin Ashi

📓 From your notes

Q5: Japanese candlesticks. Ans: Heiken Aishi.

🛠 Spelling and meaning

The usual spelling is Heikin Ashi (Japanese for "average bar"). It is a variation of Japanese candlesticks, not the same thing. It averages prices to filter out noise.

HA Close = (Open + High + Low + Close) ÷ 4 HA Open = (previous HA Open + previous HA Close) ÷ 2 HA High = highest of (High, HA Open, HA Close) HA Low = lowest of (Low, HA Open, HA Close)

Use Heikin Ashi to...

See the trend at a glance, stay in a trade during a strong move, avoid panicking on one red candle.

Do not use it to...

Place entries, stops or zones. HA prices are averages, not real traded prices. FVGs, order blocks and wicks must be read on normal candles.

4The 5 candle rules from your notes

  1. Compare body with wick, it matters.Body = who won. Wick = where someone got rejected.
  2. Never lose the context of the market.Trend, zone, premium/discount and liquidity give a candle its meaning.
  3. Candles on HTF matter more than LTF (most important).A daily rejection wick involves far more money than a 1 minute one. Read the big candle first.
  4. Listen to the market, it shows an art (kala).Reading candles is a skill built by screen time. Watch how price behaves at levels, not just where it ends up.
  5. Price itself is the live indicator.Indicators are calculated from price and lag behind it. Raw price action is the fastest information you have.

Why the higher timeframe candle wins

✅ Key takeaways
  • Body shows who won, wicks show rejection. Compare them.
  • Candle signals only count in context (zone, trend, liquidity).
  • Higher timeframe candles carry more weight.
  • Heikin Ashi is great for seeing trend, but use real candles for levels and entries.
