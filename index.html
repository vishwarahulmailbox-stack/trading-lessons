The whole method in one story

  1. 1Price moves because of liquidityOrders are the fuel. Price swings between balance (range) and imbalance (strong moves).
  2. 2Read the structureIs it trending up, down, or ranging? Where are the highs and lows? Is this a pullback or a reversal?
  3. 3Go top downHTF tells you who is in control, MTF tells you where to enter, LTF tells you when.
  4. 4Find the zoneSupply and demand, order blocks, fair value gaps and flip zones, located in discount (buys) or premium (sells).
  5. 5Know where the money isLiquidity above highs and below lows: it is both a trap to avoid and a target to aim for.
  6. 6Trade at the right timeKill zones and a clear daily bias with an invalidation level.
  7. 7Protect the accountFixed risk per trade, stop below the zone, target at liquidity, process before profit.
