TRADER'S NOTEBOOK : Price action tutorial site
==============================================

How to open: unzip the folder and double-click index.html. It runs in any modern
browser with no installation and no internet needed (fonts load from Google when
online; without internet the site falls back to system fonts).

Contents
  index.html                Home, roadmap and the trading pyramid
  17 lesson pages           One per topic in the notes (Q1 to Q17)
  cheat-sheet.html          Full process, golden rules, master checklist, glossary
  assets/style.css          Styles (light and dark theme)
  assets/charts.js          All charts and interactive diagrams (plain SVG)
  assets/app.js             Navigation, quizzes and lesson progress

Progress and theme are saved in your browser only.
Educational content, not financial advice.
